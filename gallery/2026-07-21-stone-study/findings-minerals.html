Minerals, stone & crystals — how geology writes a texture

Five study sessions (stone/rock, crystal properties, gem colour, geography, ores) compiled into one craft reference: real geology → pixel levers, for T.N.A.'s Arcane Stone and crystals.

Pixel-Art-Aide studio · 2026-07-21 · real geology + Minecraft/mod idioms · study only, no pixels reused

The one idea. Geologists classify every rock by exactly two things — texture (grain) and composition (chemistry) — and those map straight onto a pixel artist's two dials: arrangement/busyness and palette. Add a third axis, structure (how it formed), and you can derive a believable stone, crystal, or ore from first principles instead of guessing.

The five sessions

S Stone & rock

  • Rock = texture + composition; those are our two dials.
  • Grain→cluster size; felsic pale / mafic dark / iron warm; structure = speckle/bands/veins.
  • Vanilla's stone family = one speckle grammar recoloured by value+hue.
  • Thaumcraft Arcane Stone = a dressed, quarried-by-wizards stone (bricks/stairs/slabs).

1 Crystal properties

  • Habit sets silhouette: prismatic shard · tabular plate · botryoidal lobes · drusy crust · radiating geode.
  • Cleavage = flat straight facet edges; conchoidal fracture (quartz/obsidian) = curved chips.
  • Luster sets the highlight (metallic / vitreous / adamantine / dull).
  • Diaphaneity = the alpha budget.

2 Gem colour & optics

  • Hue = a trace chromophore on a colourless base; hue is a free design choice.
  • Idiochromatic (colour throughout) vs allochromatic (tinted clear body).
  • Brilliance = a bright/dark facet MIX; at 16 px prefer few large "step-cut" facets + 1–2 sparkle points.
  • Clarity separates polished gem from raw crystal.

3 Geography & landforms

  • Layered rock reads as horizontal bands (tilt/cross-bed for drama).
  • Differential weathering: hard rock → cliffs/hoodoos + sharp texture; soft → slopes + rounded.
  • Cave speleothems are their own family (anthodite = the radiating habit).
  • Geode = dark host rind + inward radiating crystal points; deposits grow in concentric rings.

4 Ores & veins

  • Ore = mineral clusters ON a copy of the host rock (the accent, not the block).
  • Hosting = the arrangement: vein / disseminated / massive.
  • Ore colour codes the mineral; a pale quartz gangue halo sells "natural deposit".
  • Contrast + sharp edges = ore readability; subtle glow for magic ores only.

Applied — Arcane Stone & arcane crystals (T.N.A.)

▪ Arcane Stone

  • A refined dressed ashlar — cool blue-grey, mid value (~40–60%), low busyness, fine speckle.
  • Family on one locked palette: raw → bricks (tidy mortar) → chiseled/runed → pillar; variation structural.
  • Faint engraved runes on the runed variant only; teal #7FE8D8 reserved for that emissive accent.
  • Base can lean subtle foliation/vein (quarried-by-wizards) or fine even speckle — try both.

◆ Arcane / vis crystal

  • Geode pocket or anthodite cluster: dark host rind + inward radiating teal facets.
  • Teal is the chromophore colour-code; a bright/dark facet mix gives the sparkle.
  • "Magic glow" = the real UV-fluorescent calcite hook; keep the emissive sparse.
  • Aura-crystal growth reads as concentric deposited banding (rings), like agate / tree end-grain.
