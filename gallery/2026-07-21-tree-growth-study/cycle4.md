# Cycle 4 — Magic / fantasy mod trees: how lore-driven form departs from natural growth

Scope: Thaumcraft greatwood & silverwood (T.N.A.'s direct ancestors), Botania livingwood/dreamwood,
Ars Nouveau archwood — where magic *exaggerates* or *overrides* the natural growth rules of cycles 1–3.

## Findings
- **Silverwood (Thaumcraft):** tall magical tree, **pale silver wood, sparkling blue leaves**, thick
  trunk in a **3×3 plus-shape tapering to a single block** at the top. **Rare** — common only in
  Magical Forest, very rare elsewhere, spawns in any biome. Crucially: **most silverwoods generate a
  PURE AURA NODE in their heartwood**, which converts the surrounding area into Magical Forest and
  reverts taint *while still in the wood block*. The tree literally **seeds its own biome** from its core.
- **Greatwood (Thaumcraft):** **thick 2×2 trunk**, vertical, with **long straight branches radiating
  in all directions** tipped with thick leaf clusters; grows **mammoth** (trunk 11–23 blocks). Relatively
  **common** (~4% chunk chance), grows almost anywhere. The classic old-growth giant.
- **Botania livingwood → dreamwood:** magic wood obtained by **transformation** (Pure Daisy transmutes
  a log → livingwood; livingwood through the Alfheim portal → pale ethereal dreamwood), not by normal
  growth. Magic = a transmuted state of ordinary wood.
- **Ars Nouveau archwood:** **four dramatically colourful varieties** (Vexing/Blazing/Cascading/
  Flourishing), rare spawn in any biome, magical fruits, its own rare **Archwood Forest** biome full of
  magical lights.

## Growth→shape lessons
1. **Magic trees carry their power in the CORE/heartwood, not just the canopy.** Silverwood grows a pure
   aura node in its heart. → the end-grain/core is the magical focus, and *should read as holding
   something*. (Directly validates our teal-heart / halo silverwood **core** candidates — they're
   lore-correct, not decoration.)
2. **Trunk cross-section is a species signature.** Silverwood = 3×3 plus tapering to 1; greatwood = 2×2
   straight; vanilla = 1×1 or 2×2. The trunk **footprint/shape** is an identity axis independent of texture.
3. **Fantasy exaggerates ONE natural axis, over a believable structure.** Dramatic colour (archwood),
   luminosity (magical lights), pale ethereal (dreamwood), sparkling leaves (silverwood) — but the growth
   form underneath stays legible. Magic is an **accent layer on real form** (ties to "reserve emissive
   glow for the truly magical").
4. **A magic tree can be an ecological ANCHOR, not just a plant.** Silverwood's node converts its
   surroundings to Magical Forest; archwood defines an Archwood Forest. The rare special tree **seeds and
   spaces its own grove** rather than packing a common forest.
5. **Warm-common-giant vs cool-rare-special is a real, lore-backed pairing:** greatwood (common, mammoth,
   2×2, radiating branches, grows anywhere) vs silverwood (rare, node-hearted, pale, blue-sparkle leaves,
   grove-seeding, spaced). Exactly T.N.A.'s warm/cool pair.

## Implication for T.N.A. (strong, lore-grounded)
- **Silverwood core = a heart that holds a node** → the teal-heart / halo cores are canon, not flourish;
  keep the luminous centre. Leaves "sparkling blue" → our teal-shimmer + frost (icy) leaf direction is on-theme.
- **Greatwood = 2×2 mammoth old-growth**, radiating branches, common everywhere — the warm giant.
- **Silverwood = rarer, pale, spaced grove-seeder**, 3×3-plus tapering trunk — the cool special payoff.
