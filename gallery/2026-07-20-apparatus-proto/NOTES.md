# Arcane apparatus prototype (2026-07-20) — Part-B experiment

Applies the mod-design-study north star to an original in-theme block:
- Restrained NEUTRAL material (cool dressed arcane blue-grey stone) — Create/Mekanism
  "apparatus is a material, distinguished from brass by hue".
- Framed recessed panel + beveled border + corner rune-studs — Create framed-panel grammar,
  studs in aetherium (our metal) so the magic reads as the accent.
- Central EMISSIVE aspect socket in the teal glint #7FE8D8 — Embers/Malum "emissive core =
  active/charged", the only saturated thing on the block.
Original pixels (make_apparatus.py is the source). Rendered iso via aide.blockrender.
Proof-of-concept that the study feeds real design: a casing that sits beside Create
without copying its brass. Next candidates: essentia conduit (flow motes), tiered
altar (Astral marble + engraved-rune tiers), crucible-as-gauge (Blood Magic fluid level).
