# Mod design study (2026-07-20)

Studied 18 highly-downloaded mods adding UI/blocks/mobs/effects, via 6 parallel
research agents. Reference policy: design idioms only — no mod pixels/models/asset
files fetched, traced, or reproduced. Licenses recorded per mod (all study-only).

Deliverable: findings.html — collapsible <details> per category (page stays short),
per-mod cards (Meta/Techniques/Takeaways/Signature + license badge), and a North-Star
synthesis up top. Source prose in findings-raw.md.

Mods: Ars Nouveau, Botania, Occultism, Malum, Iron's Spellbooks (magic);
Supplementaries, Macaw's, FramedBlocks (decorative/wood-set); Create, Embers,
Mekanism (effects/apparatus); Alex's Mobs, Mowzie's, Cataclysm (mobs);
JEI/REI, Jade, Patchouli, Ars Nouveau UI (UI/guidebook).

Directly feeds: the aspect icon system (per-aspect hue → icons+particles+UI),
apparatus block grammar (neutral material + framed panels + emissive cores),
the Codex/worktable GUIs, and the wood-set completion (next task).
