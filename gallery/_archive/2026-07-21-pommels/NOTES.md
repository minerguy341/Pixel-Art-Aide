# Pommels (2026-07-21)

The counter-weight knob at the BUTT of the wand — the other end from the tip
cap. Types follow the Oakeshott pommel families (wheel/disc, spherical, pear,
scent-stopper, crescent) plus an onion/mushroom knob. A pommel attaches by a
short shaft stub, not the ferruled collar the tip caps use.

| Pommel | Lift | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| A-wheel | revolve | flat disc / wheel pommel |
| B-sphere | revolve | spherical (oblate) knob |
| C-pear | revolve | pear / conical pommel |
| D-scent-stopper | hybrid · poly-6 | faceted bottle-shaped stopper |
| E-crescent | hybrid · blade | crescent pommel |
| F-mushroom | revolve | onion / mushroom dome |

Silhouettes via `gallery/capkit.py`; recipes in `build_pommels.py`. Verified
headless: each is one connected 3D piece (C-pear needed a stem to weld its
tapered neck to the shaft); iso previews in `previews/`.

Sources studied (general craft, nothing committed): Oakeshott pommel typology
and medieval-sword pommel guides (wheel/disc, scent-stopper, pear, crescent).
